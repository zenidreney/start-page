# A stylish Start-Page for your phone, tablet or desktop.

Just open it in your favorite browser go to settings and set it as homepage.

This project was an opportunity for me to work with some simple APIs. I have written them in diffetent syntax just to
get confortable with each one (.then async etc.). I am aware to use syntax consistently in a larger project.

The background image is from Lorem Picsum(https://picsum.photos/).

I have not added geo-location on purpose to keep it simple and to the point. So, just enter the city where your want the
Open-Meteo API(https://open-meteo.com/en/docs).

Time will automatically sync to your local through your device.

The current search is through DuckDuckGo(https://duckduckgo.com/).

The quotes are from dummyJSON(https://dummyjson.com/docs/quotes).
